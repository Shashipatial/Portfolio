Get To Know Me

An Educator Committed to Excellence

Dedicated to shaping confident, knowledgeable business graduates through conceptual clarity, ethical values, and practical learning.

I am A.P. Shashi Patial, Assistant Professor of BBA at Government Degree College, Indora. My academic foundation in commerce and education — including an M.Com, B.Ed, and a Diploma in Computer Applications — has equipped me with both subject expertise and pedagogical insight. I am also a UGC NET qualified academic, reflecting my commitment to continuous learning and subject mastery.

My teaching experience spans Pathankot College of Education and GDC Indora, where I have worked to build conceptual understanding, encourage interactive learning, and support students in their academic and professional growth. My interests lie in business administration, management education, accounting, and student development.

I believe in creating a classroom environment that values conceptual clarity, ethical practices, and practical application. My goal is to help students develop the confidence and capability to succeed in an evolving business world.

Great teaching inspires curiosity, builds confidence, and empowers students to turn knowledge into opportunity.
